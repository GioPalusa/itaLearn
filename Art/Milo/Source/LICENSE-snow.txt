Milo is based on Snow v4.2 by Blender Studio.

Snow is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for LangLearn include a reduced runtime skeleton, baked facial
controls, material conversion, retargeted motion clips, and the name Milo.

Copyright and attribution for the original Snow character remain with
Blender Studio: https://studio.blender.org/

Additional roughness, normal and skin subsurface maps are derived from the
Blender Studio snow_barscene_pack (CC BY 4.0). They have been resized and skin
UDIM tiles have been combined for the runtime UV atlas. See surface-sources.json
for exact source hashes. The studio lighting environment is authored for Milo.
